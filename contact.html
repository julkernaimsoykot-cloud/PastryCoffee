Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We offer same-day delivery across Dhaka. Simply place your order through our Order page, pay via bKash, Nagad, or cash on delivery, and we'll bring Milestone to your door.

Can I book a table in advance?

Absolutely. Call us at 01923767687 or send us a message through the contact form above and we'll arrange a reserved table for you and your guests.

Do you do custom cakes for events?

Yes! Our pastry team creates stunning custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, corporate events, and any celebration. Contact us at least 3 days in advance for custom orders.

Are there vegetarian/vegan options?

Many of our coffee drinks can be made with oat, almond, or soy milk. Several pastries are vegetarian-friendly. Please speak with our staff for specific dietary requirements.
