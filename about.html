Our Origin

A Story Brewed in Passion

Founded with a singular vision — to create an extraordinary coffee and pastry experience in Bangladesh — Milestone Coffee & Pastry Shop opened its doors with the dream of bringing the elegance of European café culture to Dhaka.

Every element of our shop, from the carefully sourced single-origin coffee beans to the handcrafted pastries made fresh each morning, reflects our commitment to quality. We partner with premium roasters and local artisan bakers to deliver an experience that's truly one of a kind.

Proudly connected with Milestone College, we represent a community of excellence, creativity, and aspiration — values that are baked into everything we do.
