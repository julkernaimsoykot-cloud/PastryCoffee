Our Story

Crafted with Passion, Served with Love

Milestone Coffee & Pastry Shop was born from a passion for exceptional coffee and handcrafted pastries. Nestled in the heart of Bangladesh, we bring the warmth and elegance of a luxury café to every guest who walks through our doors.

From expertly sourced single-origin beans to our delicate artisan pastries baked fresh each morning, every detail is designed to create a memorable experience. We believe great coffee is an art — and we're dedicated to perfecting it every single day.
